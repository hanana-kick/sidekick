# Built-in demo visual assets

These assets are served locally by the three fictional Sidekick example sites. They do not introduce third-party runtime requests, analytics, or user tracking.

## NOVA (`company-*`)

Original AI-generated fictional audio product photography, created for this demo. NOVA is a fictional brand; the depicted hardware, specifications, and offers are illustrative. The assets do not use Apple marks or real Apple product photographs.

## MUSE (`shop-*`)

Original AI-generated fashion editorial and catalog photography, created for this demo. Product names, stores, prices, promotions, and reviews are fictional. Model imagery does not imply a real endorsement.

## THREAD (`community-*`)

Locally vendored illustrative photographs. Source: Unsplash, under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The source image identifiers are listed below. Community identities, posts, and engagement counts are fictional and are not associated with the photographers or people pictured.
