# MUSE fashion imagery

The 13 WebP images in this set were generated for this fictional storefront using OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-21. They are original AI-generated demo assets, not photographs of real listed merchandise and not copied from Zigzag or another store. There are no third-party stock-photo license or attribution requirements attached to this asset set. Use is subject to the applicable OpenAI terms; no separate third-party copyright license is asserted.

- shop-hero.webp: editorial fashion photo of an adult model wearing a neutral oversized blazer and blue denim, seated by a plaster wall. Generated as a standalone photo and converted to WebP.
- shop-product-0.webp through shop-product-11.webp: generated 4 × 3 contact sheet of adult fashion models, a shoulder bag and ballet shoes. Sliced at actual visual cell boundaries, with separators removed, then converted to WebP. Products 12–23 are clearly named Studio Edition variants using the corresponding style imagery.

All imagery is served locally; there are no runtime image requests to external services. The initial exploratory stock image was discarded and is not used in the implementation.
